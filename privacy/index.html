Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026 · OnlineMeasurer.com

At OnlineMeasurer.com (producers of the Naapu measurement game and digital ruler tool), your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy discloses what data is generated when you access our website, how we utilize client-side storage technologies, and how third-party advertising vendors utilize cookies.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike many web services, OnlineMeasurer does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide your real name, address, or telephone number. We do not maintain a server-side relational database of individual gameplay sessions or personal profiles.

2. Use of Browser LocalStorage

To deliver a seamless offline-capable utility without requiring remote server authentication, Naapu stores operational parameters directly inside your browser's private window.localStorage. This data includes:

  • Screen Calibration (naapu_calib): Stores your display's calculated pixels-per-millimeter factor (pxPerMm) so you do not have to recalibrate on every visit.
  • Game Progress & Records (naapu_save): Saves your unlocked worlds, stars earned, experience points (XP), player rank, and daily challenge streak count.
  • Audio & Vibration Preferences: Remembers whether you have toggled sound synthesizer effects or haptic feedback on or off.
  • Ad Consent Status: Records whether you have accepted or customized advertising consent where applicable.

Crucial Note: All localStorage items remain exclusively on your device. They are never transmitted to our web servers, never sold, and can be cleared instantly at any time by clearing your browser cache or site data.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Cookies

To keep our tools free for users worldwide, OnlineMeasurer.com works with third-party advertising partners, notably Google AdSense.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies and web beacons to serve advertisements on this website.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) enables it and its network partners to serve ads to users based on their prior visits to this site and other websites across the Internet.
  • Third-party ad networks or vendors may also use cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons to gauge the effectiveness of their advertisements and to personalize advertising content. OnlineMeasurer does not have direct access to or control over cookies placed by third-party advertisers.

4. How to Opt Out of Personalized Advertising

You have the complete freedom to opt out of personalized (interest-based) advertising at any time:

  • Google Ads Settings: You can manage or disable personalized advertising served by Google by visiting the official Google Ads Settings Page.
  • Network Advertising Initiative & Digital Advertising Alliance: You can opt out of a wide array of third-party vendor cookies across the web by visiting AboutAds.info Choices or Your Online Choices (for European visitors).
  • Browser Cookie Controls: You can configure your browser to reject all cookies, alert you when a cookie is placed, or clear existing cookies upon browser close. Please refer to your browser's help documentation (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).

5. Web Analytics

If lightweight web analytics are active, they operate in strict privacy-friendly mode: recording aggregate non-identifiable events (such as page views or tool usage milestones) without tracking unique personal identities across domains, without fingerprinting, and without storing cross-site tracking cookies.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

OnlineMeasurer is designed for general audiences and educational measurement practice. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory enhancements. Any changes will be posted immediately on this page with an updated Effective Date.
