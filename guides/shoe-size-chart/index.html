Shoe Size Chart: UK, India, US and EU Sizes with Foot Length

Updated: November 2026 · Reading time: 6 mins

Shoe sizes are confusing because every region numbers them differently. India uses the UK system, the US adds one to one-and-a-half sizes, and Europe uses a completely separate scale. The one number that never changes is the length of your foot in centimetres, so this guide starts there.

How to Measure Your Foot at Home

  1. Put a sheet of A4 paper on a hard floor, with one short edge touching a wall.
  2. Stand on the paper with your heel against the wall and your weight on that foot.
  3. Ask someone to mark the tip of your longest toe (it is not always the big toe).
  4. Measure from the wall edge of the paper to the mark in centimetres. Do the same for the other foot and use the longer one.
  5. Measure in the evening: feet swell slightly during the day, and shoes bought for morning feet can feel tight later.

An A4 sheet is 29.7 cm long, so it is long enough for almost every adult foot. If you do not have a tape measure, the online cm ruler can measure the mark once your screen is calibrated, or you can compare against a credit card (8.56 cm long).

Why Sizes Don't Match Exactly Between Brands

Shoe size systems were built on different units: UK and US sizes step in thirds of an inch (about 8.5 mm), while EU "Paris points" step in two-thirds of a centimetre (about 6.7 mm). Because the steps are different, a UK size never lines up perfectly with one EU size, and each brand rounds a little differently. That is why the same person can wear EU 42 in one brand and EU 43 in another.

  • Between two sizes? Choose the larger one for closed shoes and boots, the smaller one for sandals.
  • Wide feet? Length is only half the story. Many Indian and sports brands offer wide fittings; try a half size up if a brand runs narrow.
  • Leave a thumb's width (about 1 cm) between your longest toe and the front of the shoe for walking and running.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Indian shoe size the same as UK size?

Yes. Most Indian brands and stores use UK sizes, so an Indian size 8 is a UK size 8, roughly a US men's 9 and EU 42.

How do I convert UK to US shoe size?

For men, add 1 to the UK size (UK 8 = US 9). For women, add about 2 (UK 5 = US 7). Always check the brand's own chart because rounding varies.

What foot length is shoe size 8?

A men's UK/India size 8 fits a foot of about 26.2 cm (10.3 inches). A women's UK size 8 is the same length, about 26.2 cm.

Should I measure my foot in the morning or evening?

In the evening. Feet swell a little during the day, so an evening measurement gives a size that stays comfortable.
