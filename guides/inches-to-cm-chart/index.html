Inches to Centimeters (cm) Conversion Chart & Formulas

Published: October 2026 · Reading Time: 5 mins · Topic: Unit Conversion & Dimensional Calculations

Converting between imperial inches (in or ″) and metric centimeters (cm) is one of the most frequent dimensional calculations in carpentry, 3D printing, clothing sizing, graphic design, and engineering. Under international metrological law, the relationship between inches and centimeters is mathematically exact: 1 inch is defined as exactly 25.4 millimeters, or 2.54 centimeters.

The Exact Conversion Formulas

Because the international inch is formally anchored to the SI meter, there is zero rounding error in the conversion factor:

  • Inches to Centimeters: Length in cm = Length in inches × 2.54
  • Inches to Millimeters: Length in mm = Length in inches × 25.4
  • Centimeters to Inches: Length in inches = Length in cm / 2.54 (or approximately cm × 0.393701)
  • Millimeters to Inches: Length in inches = Length in mm / 25.4

How to Convert Fractions of an Inch to Centimeters

In American architecture and woodworking, lengths are commonly written in fractions such as 5 ⅜ inches. To convert any fraction to centimeters:

  1. Convert Fraction to Decimal: Divide the numerator by the denominator. For example, for 3/8: 3 / 8 = 0.375.
  2. Add Whole Inches: Add the whole number: 5 + 0.375 = 5.375 inches.
  3. Multiply by 2.54: Multiply the decimal sum by 2.54: 5.375 × 2.54 = 13.6525 cm (or 136.5 mm).

Practical Measurement Applications

Understanding both units prevents costly mistakes across common scenarios:

  • Display Screen Sizes: Televisions and laptops are sold by diagonal inch rating (e.g., a 15.6-inch laptop or 55-inch TV). A 15.6-inch diagonal equals 15.6 × 2.54 = 39.62 cm.
  • Apparel & Body Measurements: Pants waists, inseams, and collar sizes specified in inches (e.g., 32-inch waist) equal 32 × 2.54 = 81.28 cm.
  • Mailing & Packaging Boxes: International shipping carriers often charge volumetric dimensional weight calculated in centimeters (L × W × H in cm / 5000). Always convert box dimensions from inches to centimeters before calculating postage fees. Check your package in our 2D Fit Checker.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the exact formula to convert inches to centimeters?

Multiply the length in inches by exactly 2.54. Formula: cm = inches × 2.54. For millimeters, multiply by 25.4.

Why is one inch exactly equal to 2.54 centimeters?

In 1959, national metrology institutes from the US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, and South Africa signed the International Yard and Pound Treaty, tying the international yard to exactly 0.9144 meters, making 1 inch equal to exactly 25.4 mm (2.54 cm).

How do you quickly estimate centimeters to inches in your head?

A quick mental rule of thumb is to divide the centimeter value by 2.5, or multiply centimeters by 4 and shift the decimal place one spot to the left (e.g. 10 cm × 4 = 40 → approx 4.0 inches; exact is 3.94 in).
