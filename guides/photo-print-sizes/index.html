Photo Print Sizes in cm and Inches (with Pixels Needed)

Updated: November 2026 · Reading time: 5 mins

Photo sizes are a mix of inches (4×6, 5×7), paper sizes (A4, A5) and official millimetre sizes (passport photos). This guide lists them all in centimetres and inches, and shows how many pixels your image needs so the print stays sharp.

How Many Pixels Do You Need?

Print quality is measured in dots per inch (DPI). At 300 DPI a print looks sharp even close up; 150 DPI is usually acceptable for prints viewed from an arm's length, like posters. To find the pixels needed, multiply the print size in inches by the DPI: a 4 × 6 inch print at 300 DPI needs 1200 × 1800 pixels.

Almost every modern phone camera takes photos of 12 megapixels or more (around 3000 × 4000 pixels), which is enough for a sharp A4 or 8 × 10 print. Images downloaded from social media or messaging apps are often compressed to 1000–1600 pixels wide, which is fine for 4 × 6 but will look soft on A4.

Aspect Ratio: Why Your Photo Gets Cropped

Phone photos are usually 4:3, while a 4 × 6 print is 3:2 and an A4 sheet is about 1:1.41. When the shapes don't match, the lab either crops the edges or adds white borders. Before printing, crop the photo to the print's shape yourself so nothing important (like the top of a head) is cut off.

Passport Photo Rules at a Glance

  • India, UK and most of Europe: 35 × 45 mm, plain light background, face covering most of the height.
  • United States: 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm), white or off-white background.
  • Always check the latest rules on the official passport or visa website before printing, as requirements on background and head size change from time to time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the size of a passport photo in India in cm?

3.5 cm wide by 4.5 cm high (35 × 45 mm). For a sharp print at 300 DPI the image should be at least 413 × 531 pixels.

What size is a 4×6 photo in cm?

A 4 × 6 inch photo is 10.16 × 15.24 cm, often rounded to 10 × 15 cm. It needs 1200 × 1800 pixels for a sharp print.

How many pixels do I need to print A4?

For a sharp A4 print at 300 DPI you need about 2480 × 3508 pixels. At 150 DPI, 1240 × 1754 pixels is the minimum for an acceptable print.

What is stamp size photo size?

Stamp size photos used on many Indian forms are 2 × 2.5 cm (20 × 25 mm). Always check the form, as some ask for a different size.
