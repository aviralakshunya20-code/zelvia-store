Play Mezur – Everyday Object Size Estimation Game

Train your estimation skills across 8 themed worlds. Each round challenges you to guess the physical millimeter dimension of an everyday object.

Game Rules & How Scoring Works

Every set consists of 5 interactive rounds. In each round, an object from the selected world is presented alongside its target measurement (such as diameter, length, or height).

  • Step 1: The Guess Phase – Use the slider and fine-tuning (+/-) buttons to set your estimate in millimeters or centimeters. Pressing "Pakka!" locks in your guess.
  • Step 2: Bonus Measurement – If you possess the physical object (such as a coin, battery, or card) and it fits on your screen, you can place it directly over your calibrated display and drag the marker jaws for bonus points.
  • Step 3: Reveal & Scoring – The exact dimension is shown alongside your estimate. High accuracy yields up to 100 points per round plus combo multipliers (up to 1.3x) for consecutive good guesses.
  • Step 4: Stars & XP – Finish 5 rounds to calculate your set score, earn up to 3 stars, gain XP, and unlock subsequent worlds.

Screen Calibration Reminder

To ensure that screen measurements and bonus rounds correspond to genuine physical dimensions, make sure your screen is calibrated. Calibration uses an ISO/IEC 7810 credit card (85.6 mm length). You can recalibrate anytime via the Settings or Ruler pages.

How to Play

  1. Calibrate your screen once with a bank card so sizes are real.
  2. Guess the size of an everyday object — a coin, a battery, a sheet of paper — by dragging on screen.
  3. Reveal the true size and score points for accuracy. Within 2% is a perfect score.
  4. Progress through 8 worlds, from pocket coins to sports courts, unlocking new items and titles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the game free?

Yes. It runs in your browser with no sign-up, and your progress is saved on your device.

Does it work offline?

After your first visit the game is cached, so most of it keeps working without a connection.

How is the score calculated?

Points depend on how close your guess is in percent. A guess within about 2% earns full points, and points fall as the error grows.
