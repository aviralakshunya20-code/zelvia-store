Tips for an accurate size

  • Use a ring that already fits the finger you are buying for, and measure the inside edge, not the outside.
  • Fingers are slightly bigger in the evening and when warm; measure at the end of the day.
  • For wide bands (over 6 mm), go up half a size.
  • If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one.
How do I find my ring size at home?

Place a ring that fits on the circle on your screen and adjust it until the circle matches the inside edge of the ring. The page then shows your US, UK and EU size. Calibrate the screen with a bank card first for an exact result.

What is the most common ring size?

For women it is around US 6 to 7 (16.5–17.3 mm inner diameter); for men around US 9 to 10 (18.9–19.8 mm).

How is ring size measured?

By the inner diameter or inner circumference of the ring. EU (ISO) sizes are simply the inner circumference in millimetres.
