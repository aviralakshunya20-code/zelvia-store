Online Fit Checker – 2D Dimension & Clearance Calculator

Determine if an object fits inside a container, envelope, or shelf space. Enter width, height, and safety margin to check fit and test 90° rotation.

How the 2D Fit Calculator Works

The Fit Checker evaluates whether an item (with given width and height) can be placed entirely inside an outer container (such as a parcel box, drawer, or luggage compartment) while honoring a designated border clearance margin.

Direct Orientation vs Rotated Fit

An object can fit in either its original orientation or rotated by 90 degrees:

  • Direct Fit: (item.width + 2 × margin ≤ box.width) AND (item.height + 2 × margin ≤ box.height).
  • Rotated 90° Fit: (item.height + 2 × margin ≤ box.width) AND (item.width + 2 × margin ≤ box.height).

Why Safety Margin Matters

In the real world, rigid objects cannot slide into containers that match their exact theoretical dimensions. Extra clearance margin is required to account for:

  • Protective Padding: Bubble wrap, corrugated cardboard, or protective foam (typically 10 mm to 25 mm).
  • Finger Clearance: Space to grasp and lift the item out of a deep drawer or box without tilting.
  • Manufacturing Variance: Slight bends, seams, or corner radii in cardboard and plastic bins.
