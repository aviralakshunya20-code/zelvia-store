Online Screen Ruler – Calibrated Millimeter & Inch Ruler

Measure small items directly on your device display. Once calibrated with any standard payment card, the interactive markings reflect true 1:1 physical dimensions.

How to Use the On-Screen Ruler

  1. Calibrate for exact size: The ruler works immediately at an approximate scale. For true 1:1 accuracy, click "Calibrate Now" and place an ATM, credit, or debit card over the screen. Align the card edge to the outline to configure exact pixel scaling.
  2. Place the Object: Place your coin, key, screw, or card flat against the device display. Align one edge with the zero mark (or left jaw).
  3. Drag the Measurement Jaws: Touch or click-and-drag the orange pointer to span the object's width. The readout displays the exact measurement instantly.
  4. Switch Units: Toggle between mm (millimeters), cm (centimeters), and in (inches).

Accuracy and Optical Precautions

Screen rulers are accurate to approximately ±0.5 mm when calibrated correctly. Keep in mind:

  • Protective Glass: Thick screen protectors introduce a small optical gap (parallax). Always view straight from above.
  • Browser Zoom: Keep your browser zoom at 100%. If you pinch-zoom or adjust browser scale, press "Calibrate" to reset the scaling factor.
  • For in-depth details on pixel density and accuracy boundaries, read our Screen Ruler Calibration Guide.

Getting the Most Accurate Reading

  1. Calibrate once with any bank, debit or ID card. Every ISO card is exactly 85.60 mm long, so it is a perfect reference.
  2. Keep browser zoom at 100% and don't pinch the page; zooming changes the scale.
  3. Look straight down at the screen. Viewing at an angle through thick glass shifts the reading by up to half a millimetre.
  4. For long objects, measure in two parts and add the results.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an online ruler accurate?

After calibration with a bank card, readings are typically accurate to about ±0.5 mm. Without calibration the scale is only an estimate, because every screen has a different pixel density.

Why does the ruler need a card to calibrate?

Browsers do not know the physical size of your screen. Matching a card of known length (85.60 mm) lets the page work out exactly how many pixels make one millimetre on your device.

Can I use this ruler on my phone?

Yes. It works on phones, tablets and computers. Calibrate once on each device; the setting is saved in that browser.

Does it work if I change zoom or rotate the phone?

Rotating is fine. Changing browser zoom changes the scale, so the page will ask you to recalibrate.
