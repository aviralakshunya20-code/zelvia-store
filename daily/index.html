Daily Measurement Hunt – Daily Dimension Challenge

Every calendar day features a single target millimeter length. Test your unassisted perception and maintain your daily streak without using a physical ruler.

How Daily Hunt Works

The Daily Hunt is a synchronized puzzle where every player around the globe receives the same secret target dimension for any given calendar date.

  • One Opportunity: You have one official attempt each day. Adjust the slider until you believe the visual bar matches the given target length (or vice-versa).
  • Acceptance Window: A guess within 10% of the true dimension is considered a successful hit and extends your consecutive daily streak.
  • Daily Streak & Milestones: Playing on consecutive days builds your streak count. If a full calendar day is missed, the streak resets to zero, though your highest streak record is preserved in your profile.
  • Reset Time: Daily challenges update automatically at midnight according to your local device date.
